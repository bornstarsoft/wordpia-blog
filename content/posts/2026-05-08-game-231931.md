Capcom has just rolled out an exciting free DLC mini-game for Resident Evil Requiem, titled "Leon Must Die Forever." This new mode is automatically unlocked upon completing the main story, offering players a fresh challenge that diverges from the primary narrative. With fast-paced action and a variety of stages to conquer, this mini-game promises to keep players engaged long after they've finished the main campaign.

In this mode, players take on the role of Leon as they navigate through numerous stages, each designed to test their skills and reflexes. The game introduces exclusive enhancer abilities, adding layers of strategy and replay value. For those seeking an even greater challenge, multiple difficulty levels are available, including an extreme mode that only the most confident players should attempt. As the name suggests, the stakes are highcan you survive and defeat the boss within the time limit?

With the mini-game's release, fans of the franchise are encouraged to complete the main story soon, especially during Japan's Golden Week, to fully enjoy this new content. As we look forward to more updates and potential story DLC, one can't help but wonder: how will this mini-game shape the future of Resident Evil's gameplay experience?
