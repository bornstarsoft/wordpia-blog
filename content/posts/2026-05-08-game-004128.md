The Daily Mail has just launched Relink, an innovative puzzle game that challenges players to engage in lateral thinking and discover hidden connections. This daily brainteaser presents a grid of 16 words or phrases, where players must identify three words that share a common link while spotting the imposter that doesnt belong. This unique twist on traditional puzzles not only makes it entertaining but also stimulates critical thinking skills.

With a new Relink puzzle available every day at midnight, players can test their wits and compete with friends to see who can solve it first. The game encourages a blend of strategy and creativity, as players must decipher themes and wordplay to uncover the hidden connections. As you dive into each days challenge, youll find that the thrill of solving puzzles can be both satisfying and rewarding.
