Microsoft is stirring excitement in the gaming community with the rumored release of two new Xbox controllers: the Elite Series 3 and a dedicated Cloud Gaming controller. The latter, designed specifically for cloud gaming, features a compact design reminiscent of Joy-Con controllers, making it an intriguing addition for gamers who prefer a more portable option.

The Cloud Gaming controller is said to include all the standard buttons of a traditional Xbox controller but in a smaller form factor, which could revolutionize how players engage with cloud-based gaming. With a new button featuring a cloud logo, it hints at a seamless connection to games streamed over Wi-Fi, catering to users who enjoy gaming on non-traditional platforms.

On the other hand, the Elite Series 3 continues Microsofts legacy of premium controllers, now enhanced with unique features like silver scrolling wheels that may allow for quick profile switching, similar to the DualSense Edge. With a 500mAh battery and support for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi 6, this controller promises to elevate the gaming experience.

As Microsoft continues to innovate in the gaming space, it raises the question: how will these new peripherals shape the future of gaming, especially in the realm of cloud connectivity?
