Samsung's Mobile Advance (SMA) 2026 program is a game-changer for Indian startups, offering up to $50,000 in grants without taking any equity. This initiative, launched by Samsung's R&D Institutes in Bangalore and Noida, aims to foster innovation in mobile technology by collaborating with startups across various domains, including AI, health, and wearables.

The program not only provides financial support but also opens doors for mentorship from Samsung's global network and access to its extensive product ecosystem. Startups have until June 30 to submit their proposals, with the potential for long-term partnerships that could revolutionize mobile experiences worldwide. As the tech landscape evolves, how will these collaborations shape the future of mobile technology?
