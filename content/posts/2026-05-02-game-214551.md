PC gamers are in for a treat this weekend with the free demo of Nested, a vibrant puzzle game that promises both whimsy and challenge. Developed by Nathan Sioui as part of the GMTK 2024 Game Jam, Nested stands out with its unique gameplay mechanics that revolve around stacking nesting dolls to solve intricate puzzles. The games colorful aesthetic and engaging puzzles make it a perfect choice for anyone looking to unwind while exercising their brain.

In Nested, players will encounter a variety of challenges that require not just logical thinking but also creativity. The game introduces innovative mechanics such as color-matching and size-based interactions, allowing players to manipulate their environment in delightful ways. With features like Buttons that alter gameplay dynamics and Swap Nodes for switching dolls, the possibilities for puzzle-solving are nearly endless. 

If youre a fan of cozy indie games that offer a good challenge, Nested is definitely worth checking out. While the demo is available now on Steam, the full game is expected to launch in August 2026. Will you dive into the colorful world of Nested this weekend?
